# Innie World — Pro & Major College Sports Familiar Map

**Status:** planning proposal only. This document does not activate new Familiar zones, create team-affiliated characters, imply endorsement, use team logos/mascots, or authorize code changes or deployment.

## Mapping principle

Map candidate Familiar territories around the real-world home venues of professional teams and major college athletic programs. Treat these as **geographic anchors**, not as permission to copy team branding or as proof of a relationship with a team or school.

A territory should point to an original Off*Script Familiar with its own name, voice, epithet, prompt, palette, and story. Sports context can inspire regional energy, but new Familiar identities require Amber's approval.

## Geographic matching rules

1. Each approved territory uses a verified latitude/longitude anchor and an explicit radius in kilometers.
2. Team/school venue coordinates are candidates, not final centers until reviewed.
3. Multiple teams can share a stadium or arena. Records can share coordinates but must have stable distinct IDs.
4. When multiple territories match, show all eligible choices and ask which Familiar the player wants to claim. Never silently auto-select.
5. Use the existing quantized Haversine approach. Raw GPS coordinates must be discarded after matching and never be shown to other players.
6. Location permission is requested only after the player deliberately taps Locate.
7. Geographic eligibility governs first unlock only. Once unlocked, ownership is permanent and the Familiar can be equipped anywhere.
8. Existing local Field claims are not account-wide proof. Permanent ownership and cross-device selection require an approved authenticated API and server-backed state.
9. Do not require a player to enter a stadium, campus building, private property, or restricted area. Where a venue-centered radius is used, it should be safely reachable from public space and should not reward trespass or unsafe travel.

## Team data and branding boundaries

- Store team/school name, league/division, official reference URL, venue name, venue coordinates, last-verified date, and source notes as mapping metadata.
- Do not use official logos, mascots, wordmarks, uniform art, or team trade dress as Familiar artwork without permission.
- Do not imply sponsorship, partnership, licensing, or school/team endorsement.
- Familiar characters should be original Off*Script characters. A team can be a location anchor without becoming the Familiar's identity.
- Keep the feature useful for fans of every team and for players who do not follow sports.

This is a planning schema only. It must not be wired into the app without a separate go-ahead and a shared-state design review.
